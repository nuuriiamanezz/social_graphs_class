Go nuts with your LLM 🚀 Week 1 · Bootstrap edition

Our little corner of the Marvel hyperlink universe

Every week this course hands us the same 303 characters from Wikipedia's Category:Marvel Comics superheroes and a new tool. We poke at it, make something, and write down what we found. This is where that lands — eight posts, one dataset, growing all semester.

The playground

303 Marvel characters, one node each, and a directed edge whenever one character's Wikipedia article links to another's — 1,784 links in total, harvested straight from the wiki-source [[Page name]] syntax. Dense, hub-riddled, and frozen as a shared snapshot so every group in the class computes on identical data. This half of the course we mine its structure; from week 5, its text.
